About the app

NoBrick Autopost is a small automated tool run by this channel. It renders each video from scratch (every level, color and ending is generated by code) and uploads it to the channel's own YouTube and X accounts on a schedule. It is not a public service: it only ever posts to accounts owned by No Brick Left Behind, and it does not collect information about viewers.
